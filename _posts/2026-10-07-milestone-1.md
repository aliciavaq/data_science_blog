We could implement a function that works with 4 numbers, for exemple 1551 means there's 1 goalie and 5 players for the away team and 5 players and 1 goalie on the ice for the home team. That function could turn those 4 numbers into columns like players away, players home on ice... 
2) The first additional feature that could be added would be a rebound shot. We could be checking if a shot occurs shortly after a previous shot or a save by the goalie, if the interval time was very short, per example 3 seconds. Also, the distance between their coordinates could help distinguish rebounds from another shot.
Another feature could be the shot angle and distance because a shot done closer to the net and from a central position have a better chance to end in a goal. It would be calculate with the coordinates and location of the goals.
Finally, rush shots could be identified by examining the sequences of events. If a team gain possession and enters the offensive zone could be classified as a rush shot.
